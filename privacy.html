Internal Data Processing & Privacy Policy

Data Controller: POL-ENSA Sp. z o. o. • Contact: marcin.kamo@gmail.com

1. Scope & Purpose of Processing

This policy describes how DobbyFetch — a private, internal Business Intelligence (BI) and Data Warehouse (DWH) platform operated exclusively by POL-ENSA Sp. z o. o. — collects, stores, and protects data retrieved from the organization's own e-commerce channels (including Allegro, Shopify), advertising platforms (including Google Ads, Meta, GA4), customer support tools (Responso), and internal ERP/WMS systems (Subiekt GT, Feniks WMS).

DobbyFetch is used strictly by authorized internal employees (~10 users) for:

  • Internal sales, revenue, and fulfillment analytics;
  • Inventory level monitoring and restocking optimization;
  • Commission, advertising spend, and net profitability (COGS/margin) reconciliation;
  • Customer support SLA and operational performance reporting.

2. Legal Basis (GDPR / RODO)

Where operational datasets contain personal data (such as order identifiers or customer support metadata associated with transactions conducted with POL-ENSA Sp. z o. o.), processing is performed by POL-ENSA Sp. z o. o. as the Data Controller under:

  • Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: Legitimate interest of the merchant organization in internal business analytics, inventory management, fraud/anomaly detection, and operational efficiency;
  • Article 6(1)(c) GDPR: Compliance with statutory accounting, tax, and financial reporting obligations.

3. Data Minimization & Categories of Data

DobbyFetch ingests only the operational records required for internal reporting via read-only API endpoints (e.g., Allegro /order/events, /order/checkout-forms, /billing/billing-entries, /sale/offers). Analytical reporting views (analytics_generic and analytics_restricted) aggregate transactional metrics (SKUs, quantities, values, fees, fulfillment states) and minimize exposure of personal data in day-to-day dashboards and queries.

4. Strict Prohibition on Data Sharing & Commercialization

  • No Third-Party Sharing or Resale: Data processed in DobbyFetch is never sold, rented, licensed, or disclosed to external third parties or other merchants.
  • No Public Access: DobbyFetch is not a multi-tenant SaaS application and cannot be accessed by external users.
  • Controlled AI Interface: Internal employee AI assistants querying the warehouse via the custom MCP server operate strictly under read-only SQL AST guardrails, per-user table/column RBAC, and mandatory business-intent audit logging.

5. Technical & Organizational Security Measures

  • On-Premise Hosting & Network Isolation: All data is stored on self-hosted PostgreSQL 16 infrastructure accessible exclusively within the organization's private LAN/VPN.
  • Least-Privilege Access Control: Distinct database roles separate ingestion, transformation, standard employee analytics (excluding cost/margin data), and managerial financial reporting.
  • Authentication & Granular RBAC: Individual employee accounts protected with salted bcrypt/argon2 password hashes and granular table/column permissions.
  • OAuth Token Governance: API credentials (such as Allegro OAuth 2.0 Device Flow tokens) are stored in an isolated governance schema inaccessible to end users and refreshed automatically.
  • Immutable Audit Trail: All analytical queries and ETL connector executions are logged for internal compliance review.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Operational and financial analytical data is retained within the internal warehouse for as long as required for historical business reporting and statutory accounting/tax retention periods. System and query audit logs are automatically pruned by a scheduled daily cleanup job (default retention: 5 years / 1,825 days or 30 GB maximum size). Upon termination of any API integration, associated OAuth tokens are immediately revoked and removed.

7. Contact & Data Protection Inquiries

Questions regarding this policy, API data handling, or data subject rights under GDPR / RODO may be directed to the system administrator at marcin.kamo@gmail.com.
